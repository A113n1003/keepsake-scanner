# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Keepsake Scanner is committed to a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free community. We value empathy, respectful disagreement, constructive feedback, accountability, and decisions that benefit the project and its users.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and professional.
- Respect different backgrounds, abilities, identities, viewpoints, and levels of experience.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Protect other people’s privacy, especially photographs and metadata.

Harassment, sexualized conduct, threats, trolling, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue a private warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant when necessary. Decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and protect the reporter’s confidentiality.

Report an incident privately through the repository’s Security tab. Title the report “Code of Conduct report” and do not post sensitive details publicly.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) and its community impact guidelines.
